# SynapticContinuum — A Candidate Theory of Evolving Predictive Dynamics

**Living theoretical synthesis | 2026-10-30 | HYPOTHESIS, not validated architecture or claim of consciousness**

Start with [VISION.md](VISION.md) for the aspiration, [FOUNDATIONS.md](FOUNDATIONS.md) for constraints, and [ATLAS.md](ATLAS.md) for competing hypotheses and tests. This document translates the 2026-10-24 nonlinguistic/open-ended vision and the subsequent October discussions into one **idealized, revisable conceptual architecture**. It is not a specification to build all modules, and a system satisfying the vision might use completely different mechanisms. The [discussion-to-hypotheses record](notes/2026-10-30-predictive-dynamics-synthesis.md) preserves the conversational origin and unresolved caveats.

## 1. North star, and what this theory does not claim

Can an individual computational system acquire an experience-grounded inner organization in which *multiple possible developments* arise, interact, compete, combine, disappear, or reappear; in which the processes governing their significance also develop; and in which these endogenous developments have demonstrable causal effects on later beliefs, learning, attention, action, or communication?

The hypothesis is not that "all computation is prediction" or "prediction proves thinking." Evaluating f(x) is computation, but need not be a prediction about the world. A predictive process is one whose state is used to anticipate, discriminate, reconstruct, or act on uncertain consequences, with evidence for that role independent of our naming. Many intelligent computations can be viewed predictively without reducing intelligence to next-token prediction. Contemporary language models already exhibit sophisticated, high-dimensional computation; token-serialized chain-of-thought is one way to allocate additional compute, not the only possible structure.

No claim is made that an architecture implementing this theory would be conscious, sentient, superior to LLMs, biologically accurate, or original in each component. Neither perpetual firing nor an elaborate narrative decoder counts as cognition. A strong simpler baseline may reproduce the phenomena; if so the theory must change.

## 2. Evolving activity is not a prewritten thought-chain

The proposed inner process is a **dynamical field of overlapping, interacting, possibly distributed activities**, not an array of word-labeled threads. Any local activity might influence several other activities; several weak influences might jointly cross an effective threshold; some activity may be selectively inhibited; some may be reactivated after a delay. Recurrence, feedback and nonlinear interaction permit branching and convergence without pre-enumerating all branches.

What appears *afterward* as a coherent thought-chain leading to an outcome may be a convenient **retrospective compression** of a complex causal history. The actual contributing history may be a subgraph with convergent causes, unobserved competitors and feedback. Recorded correlation or an interpretable path is not causal attribution: interrupt or transplant candidate intermediate states while preserving observations and matched resources, and compare the downstream outcome. Some reasoning may genuinely be sequential, and a mixture of serial bottlenecks and distributed activity is entirely admissible.

A single node need not be a "concept," nor one concept a node. Distributed codes, transient bindings, sparse subsets, recurrent states, symbolic variables, probabilistic beliefs, and hybrids are competing realizations. The distinction between *logical concurrency*, *different update rates*, and *parallel hardware execution* must be preserved: none implies the others.

## 3. Three distinct kinds of persistence

- **Active activity:** currently influential states and interactions, whether dense, sparse, synchronous, or asynchronous.
- **Residual influence:** decaying or reactivatable traces, refractory effects, delayed messages, eligibility states, temporary associations, and intermediate-timescale context.
- **Durable learned structure:** synaptic or parameter changes, persistent associative organization, episodic memory, consolidated models, and learned modulatory policies. Long-dormant material need not stay activated for years.

A forgotten-feeling autumn breeze, smell or other partial cue might retrieve durable experience, shift affective context, and thereby cue more retrieval. This is an *illustration*, not an asserted mechanism for human autobiographical memory. Mood-congruent retrieval and odor-evoked memory have empirical precedents, including important limitations and false-memory effects (see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md)).

Time may matter through world time, internal computational steps and elapsed-time dependencies, without a mandatory global tick or physically nonstop computation. Distinct subpopulations or variables may have different or adaptive timescales. Quiescence, state checkpointing, offline consolidation and event-triggered updates remain legal. Importantly, serial token emission does not imply that Transformers have one-dimensional hidden computation.

## 4. Modulation changes the dynamics, not merely the subject

A context-sensitive **modulatory state** may alter excitation/gain, inhibition/thresholds, propagation, effective timescales, competition, plasticity, access to memories, and the allocation of finite compute. It is functionally different from an additional conscious "FEAR" or "LOVE" thought competing with dinner. It may be distributed and co-implemented with the same substrate as predictive activity; a diagrammatic separate controller is **not** mandatory.

Multiple, interacting routes may produce modulation:
1. rapid pathways from sensory cues;
2. internal/homeostatic or bodily-like signals;
3. predictions about consequences, pasts, futures, and counterfactuals;
4. residual modulatory state and learned associations;
5. possible self-prediction and interaction with other predictive processes.

A perceived danger might rapidly inhibit dinner-related activity and make rollback, backup and verification associations more accessible. A persistent mood might bias retrieval even without a consciously verbalized cause. An apparently inconsequential sensation might reactivate long-term associations and shift modulation, which then changes subsequent retrieval: activity → modulation → activity, in feedback. "Fear," "love," "bad mood," "curiosity" and "relief" are human analogies for functional effects, **not** labels to assign to machine registers or evidence of subjective feeling.

**Plausibility versus significance:** the likelihood an imagined consequence is true differs from whether investigating it matters. Low-probability high-cost possibilities can warrant attention; routine certainty need not. Modulation should be capable of adapting from experience, including correcting excessive alarms, habituation, over-suppression and rumination. Hand-designed homeostatic constraints may be useful scaffolds but must be exposed and ablated, not reported as emergent feelings.

## 5. Experience, prospective and retrospective possibility

The same evolving substrate could support predictions of near or distant futures, hypotheses about unobserved present states, retrospective explanations of pasts, counterfactuals, and retrieval or recombination of experience. These are **different epistemic statuses** even if they share mechanisms. An imagined event must not be counted as evidence, however often it is simulated. An observed creature on a table can activate fall-related analogies without being labeled "CAT"; a later linguistic label can revise an inference, not rewrite a witnessed observation.

Words can be input/output modalities and later interpretations of latent states rather than the mandatory operating clock. Language-first systems remain valid scientific controls and possible components. A readout that describes an ingenious thought is not evidence that the underlying candidate process generated it: ensure decoders and LLM critics are blind to held-out world truth and do not perform the student's reasoning.

A newly generated, never spoken possibility should matter **later**, beyond replay and statistical memorization, through experimentally measurable effects on learning, action, retention or subsequent possibilities.

## 5a. Semantic inheritance: knowing is not feeling (new candidate hypothesis, 2026-10-30)

**Key distinction: having a representation *about* sadness, fear, danger, gravity or motion is not the same as having those representations causally modify one's own dynamics.** A pretrained language or multimodal model can associate sadness with loss, social support, pessimistic interpretation, or attention shifts; this is sophisticated conceptual knowledge. It does **not** by itself establish an enduring affective state, homeostatic variable, embodied fear, or subjective feeling in the model. Conversely, a system might learn threat-sensitive gain/inhibition and altered priorities without being able to say "I am afraid." This distinction is operational: manipulate a candidate state and measure whether later propagation, retrieval, timescale, plasticity, attention or action actually changes.

### Why inheritance might be useful without being authoritative

Open-weight pretrained models already contain substantial distributed knowledge about object relationships, causes, motion, intentions, and what people *say* about emotions. These are **possible conceptual priors**, not guaranteed facts, exact executable physics, directly transferable concept nodes, or proof that the model internally experiences its descriptions. Hidden states, embeddings, elicited alternatives, sparse-autoencoder features and decoder text have different information content and failure modes.

A learner need not begin without prior structure. Biologically developing organisms possess innate constraints and learn from experience; an analogy is **inherited structure versus personally acquired experience**, not a claim that pretrained LLM weights resemble biological evolution.

Crucially, **a hypothesis need not be true to be worth considering**. A frozen model might suggest a small creature could climb, jump, fly, slip, hide, or fall, or suggest several mutually incompatible causes of an observation. Those candidates can seed *possible* internal futures or analogies, and the learner's actual observations/interventions should eventually strengthen, weaken, reject or revise them. Unexpected events may be educational; an inaccurate suggestion is not automatically a failure. Yet an *unverified possibility* is not an *observed fact*, and a misleading or harmful suggestion is not a justification for unsafe real-world action.

Distinguish four functions, even when implemented implicitly:
1. **Proposal generation:** candidate possibilities/relations suggested by pretrained knowledge or endogenous dynamics, with provenance.
2. **Belief updating:** uncertain expectations conditioned on the learner's own evidence, possibly with priors whose strength is learned. Never promote repeated speculation into evidence merely through repetition.
3. **Significance/modulation:** which possibilities merit more attention, learning, inhibition or cautious intervention; this is not identical to belief probability and cannot be inferred from semantic labels alone.
4. **Action and observation:** consequences and measurements that can discriminate predictions and provide grounded correction. A real sensor or trusted simulation oracle—not teacher prose—grounds the learner in a particular world.

If probabilities can be calibrated, Bayesian updating is one useful analogy: q(h | evidence) is influenced by a prior q(h) **and by evidence likelihood**, but the learner need not implement symbolic Bayesian inference. Many suggestions are merely *candidate sets*, not calibrated priors. Preserve probability mass or exploratory capacity for alternatives not suggested by the pretrained model.

### Four integration levels, plus an orthogonal distillation route

These are **competing experiments**, not an ordered capability ladder or roadmap requiring all four.

| Level | Pretrained source role | What persists in the learner | Main confound / failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 — occasional semantic advisor** | Frozen LLM is queried for several tentative relationships, counterfactuals or possible explanations at chosen moments | Experience, state, memory, modulation and subsequent predictions may evolve locally | The LLM may secretly be doing the substantive reasoning or leaking task truth; use teacher-absent and shuffled-suggestion controls |
| **L2 — representation borrowing** | Frozen text/multimodal encoder supplies embeddings, contextual hidden states or carefully validated learned features | Independently trained mappings, local memory and evolving latent dynamics | Geometric similarity is not causal meaning; features can be contextual, polysemantic, model-specific or misaligned with the learner's sensory world |
| **L3 — learned bidirectional bridge** | An adapter maps selected pretrained patterns into the substrate and optionally its state back to the frozen source | Local model develops its own associations, belief revision, temporal history and regulation | Adapter/teacher can dominate or encode labels/truth; isolate, swap and disconnect both bridge directions |
| **L4 — reuse neural machinery** | Frozen or partly adapted transformer blocks, learned routing, recurrence or latent feedback contribute directly to the candidate dynamics | Possibly a hybrid evolving internal state within or around pretrained layers | Instability, token-distribution mismatch, catastrophic forgetting, implicit teacher control, and greatly increased attribution difficulty |

**Distillation is a fifth, orthogonal experimental route**, not a mandatory higher level: train a compact student/adapter using a frozen model's candidate relationships or signals, then remove the teacher at evaluation. It can be paired with L1–L3 and, in principle, L4. Test whether useful relationships survive, generalize under new observations, and can be contradicted by experience rather than merely replayed.

Source compatibility matters: sentence embeddings do not necessarily expose causal relations, and internal hidden states depend on model architecture, tokenization, layer and context. Feature directions recovered by sparse autoencoders are hypotheses about model organization; interpretability does not imply a universal semantic alphabet. Open weights enable controlled probing but do not guarantee alignment with the downstream substrate.

### One optional interface in the idealized system

A **semantic inheritance port** may be connected occasionally, continuously, trained into a small student, or absent. It can feed *candidate* latent associations into the predictive field or into a proposal reservoir, but should not silently overwrite observations, assign fixed CAT/FEAR nodes or directly impose a permanent priority policy. The system's own learning and modulatory dynamics must be able to contradict and eventually ignore inherited suggestions.

There are legitimate hybrids where an LLM stays in the loop. Report these as **hybrid capabilities**, not independent endogenous cognition. Disconnection is a scientific probe, **not** a moral or architectural mandate that all abilities must survive. Likewise a pure experience-only learner is an essential baseline, not automatically the desirable final architecture.

### Critical tests before claiming useful inheritance

- **Matched alternatives:** no pretrained source; random/rotated/shuffled embeddings or candidate suggestions; generic learned representations; strong plain recurrent/state-space or explicit belief models; teacher-only and retrieval-only baselines; comparable parameter, information, training and inference budgets.
- **Grounding and counterevidence:** give the learner worlds where ordinary textbook associations are wrong. Does it update after observed counterexamples or continue copying the teacher? Compare true, incorrect-but-productive, misleading, adversarial and irrelevant suggestions, with matched novelty and confidence when possible.
- **Proposals versus proof:** label generated candidates, teacher assertions, model confidence, observed events, internal imagination, learned beliefs and external evaluator judgments distinctly. Do not let teachers or measurement decoders see held-out world truth or score the student using their own invented explanations.
- **Knowing versus modulation:** hold semantic information approximately fixed while independently intervening on its influence over gain, retrieval, inhibition, plasticity and actions. Does merely decoding "danger" differ from learned danger-sensitive reallocation that persists, calibrates and recovers?
- **Personal development:** demonstrate revision and new compositional relations learned from the learner's own interactions, perhaps under teacher disconnection, model swapping, or conflict with inherited knowledge. Look for causal traces and durable effects even if no answer is spoken.
- **Costs and failure modes:** measure teacher invocation cost, runtime energy/compute, misinformation propagation, anchoring, overconfident false memories, culturally biased priors, suppression of novel hypotheses, and regulator overreaction. In consequential environments, speculative priors must not bypass external action constraints.

This path complements rather than replaces the foundational nonlinguistic experiment: **the learner need not start as a blank slate, but it must be capable of learning what to trust and what to care about from its own experience**.

## 6. An idealized organizational picture: roles, not boxes

One *candidate* architecture may have the following **functional roles**, potentially co-located in a single recurrent network or implemented very differently:

- **Perception and action ports:** partially observable, irregular, multimodal events and interventions, plus optional language. Raw signals need not be parsed into words or preassigned objects.
- **Distributed predictive dynamics:** competing/cooperating latent processes, partial matching, relational binding, multiple possible continuations, inhibition and feedback; no prescribed verbal chain.
- **Multi-timescale retention:** active state, transient/residual history and durable learned structure, with revisable retrieval and consolidation.
- **Endogenous modulatory dynamics:** rapid and slow state-changing influences from senses, internal needs, prediction and stored history; learned gain, priority, plasticity and recovery; feedback with the predicted possibilities.
- **Epistemic/provenance discrimination:** observations versus inferred present, reconstructed past, imagined future/counterfactual and attempted versus observed actions. May be realized implicitly, not a central symbolic ledger.
- **Adaptive development:** plasticity of predictions, learned representations, learned priorities and optionally connectivity/capacity; lifelong retention, revision and novelty handling.
- **Optional inherited semantic priors / learned interface:** candidate relational possibilities from frozen LLMs, multimodal encoders, representation adapters or distilled students, kept epistemically distinct from observed experience and revisable through interaction. No required words, fixed concept nodes or teacher-authored hidden thought.
- **Selective communication, exploratory action and self/other modeling:** communicate only when useful, act to gain information, possibly predict one's own policies and another predictor's knowledge; none required at each timestep.

The roles must not be mistaken for mandatory independent modules, nor a privileged scheduler, teacher, global clock, privileged symbol table or omniscient orchestrator.

**Architecture variants to compare**: ordinary GRU/SSM or generative world model; Hopfield/episodic memory plus learned gates; temporal traces and event-driven propagation; CTM-like neuron histories and synchrony; neural cellular automata/attractor fields; explicit Bayesian/symbolic belief models; Copycat/Metacat-style stochastic codelets and slipnets; MicroPsi/Psi or intrinsic-motivation systems; hybrid formulations. A simpler competitor may win. Neural timing, synchrony, a dedicated modulator, and continual firing are each independent hypotheses, not prerequisites.

## 7. Minimal mathematical lens (optional, not a reference implementation)

Let x be active latent state, r temporary residual influences, m modulatory context, p durable learned parameters/memory, o observed events and b internal condition signals. A candidate family is:

```text
dx/dt = F(x, r, m, o; p)        (possibly event-driven or discrete)
dr/dt = R(r, x, m; p)           (multiple retention timescales)
dm/dt = G(m, x, r, o, b; p)     (direct and prediction-mediated routes)
dp/dt = U(p, x-history, r, m, observations, consequences)
```

The partitions are observational conveniences, not ontological claims. x, r, m and p might overlap or use different timescales; there may be no continuous-time differential equation in an actual implementation. Conditional gain/thresholds, gating, learned transition rates, and explicit belief distributions are alternatives. Do not assume every activation is a calibrated probability or that every internal transition predicts an external event.

An **outcome-conditioned explanatory history** is not automatically a linear sequence: H(O) may be a causal subgraph. Reconstruct candidate contributing influences, compare against counterfactual interventions on earlier states, and report when explanations are non-unique.

## 8. Phenomena and discriminating evidence

This is a theory of *potential phenomena*, not an architecture certification:

1. **Cross-history convergence:** individually weak or separated influences jointly cause a useful novel inference; trace disruption and compute-matched controls distinguish integration from replay, input shortcuts or a lookup.
2. **Heterogeneous time:** adaptive or varied timescales improve delayed interaction/transfer *beyond* recurrent states and explicit elapsed-time features under fair compute.
3. **Outcome-conditioned attribution:** multiple intermediate influences are truly necessary; a single verbalized chain misses contributing causes. Ablate or transplant different contributors, not merely visualize paths.
4. **Adaptive modulation:** external sensory, internal, and prediction-mediated pathways alter downstream possibilities and resource allocation; learned variants improve discrimination, intervention latency and recovery, not just activity count.
5. **Dormant cue reactivation:** old associations can be retrieved from partial sensory context after long silence, with retrieval biases and false-memory controls; distinguish a stable memory store from continuing activation.
6. **Self-regulation:** repeated experience changes how a system allocates and recovers from urgency/novelty-like modulation under held-out contexts.
7. **Endogenous influence:** an internally generated possibility affects later learning, self-directed sensing or behavior under fixed observations and matched compute, even if never decoded into words.
8. **Self/other prediction:** recurrent interactions among predictive processes develop useful estimates of the other process's knowledge or of one's own policy, beyond scripted game agents.

For every positive result ask: could a simpler finite-state controller, explicit event scheduler, Bayesian filter, retrieval index, recurrent neural model, world-model planner or LLM-based decoder do this with equivalent information and resources? Do not infer consciousness or a human cognitive mechanism from any toy result.

9. **Inherited possibility versus grounded belief:** pretrained proposals seed useful alternatives—including some wrong ones—while independently observed evidence changes later beliefs. Test teacher falsehoods, disagreement, disconnection, and held-out relational transfer.
10. **Knowledge versus functional modulation:** semantic knowledge of an emotion or hazard is decodable without automatically changing the model's own dynamics; separate tests identify whether learned states causally redirect attention, memory, learning or action and whether they recalibrate.

## 9. Intellectual origins and boundaries

This synthesis draws on established ideas rather than claiming to invent prediction, parallel computation, emotional modulation, associative memory, neural time or higher-order modeling.

- **Blaise Agüera y Arcas, _What Is Intelligence?_ (2025):** prediction and survival, emergent latent variables, neuromodulators, reciprocal models of self and other, and explicit challenges to simplistic dismissals of existing AI. Important speculative philosophical claims are not adopted as proved facts. [Full author-hosted text](https://whatisintelligence.antikythera.org/).
- **Darlow et al., Continuous Thought Machines (2025):** per-neuron temporal histories, synchrony and internal recurrent ticks separate from input data; meaningful published mechanisms, **not** proof of an autonomous lifetime or free-running cognitive ecology. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05522) and [research explanation](https://sakana.ai/ctm/).
- **Hofstadter/Mitchell Copycat and Metacat; MicroPsi/Psi; NARS:** fluid analogy, stochastic competing microprocesses, motives, reflective models and resource-bound inference already have serious precedents.
- **Predictive coding/active inference, recurrent/SSM world models and distributed representations:** strong alternatives; language prediction can form rich semantics and should not be caricatured as superficial autocomplete.
- **Neuroscience and memory research:** heterogeneous timescales, neuromodulation, mood-congruent recall and odor-evoked autobiographical memories motivate hypotheses but do not demonstrate an artificial mechanism.
- **LLM representation analysis and grounding:** sparse-autoencoder feature discovery, frozen-LLM embodied reinforcement learning (LLaRP), language-generated hypotheses plus environment verification (HYVIN), and surveys of grounding provide close precedents; none establishes our idealized system or that inherited features correspond to subjective feelings. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
- **Developmental predictive learning:** infant violation-of-expectation studies motivate learning from surprise and correction without equating infants with artificial learners or assuming that inaccurate predictions alone ensure learning.
- **Previous SynapticContinuum experiments:** temporal features masked trace effects (EXP-0001); traces offered limited delayed discrimination while stable activity was not semantic autonomy (EXP-0002); substrate benchmarks (EXP-0003–0005) do not validate cognition. Null results and contradictory findings must remain visible.

Related sources, limitations and primary citations are maintained in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) and [RESEARCH_LANDSCAPE.md](RESEARCH_LANDSCAPE.md).

## 10. Revision and falsification policy

Treat this as a **living explanatory synthesis**. Do not turn illustrative examples ("dinner / commit / database," "cat on a table," "autumn breeze") into hardcoded benchmark concepts or into architecture components. Capture which propositions are observations, literature, conjectures, operational tests or results. Each experiment may support, qualify, falsify, or replace a *piece* of this candidate theory; no single toy result confirms or refutes the entire vision.

If a new experimental result favors a simpler memory model, a serial architecture, no dedicated modulation, or non-predictive computation, document the contradiction and update this theory. The aspiration—experience-grounded, meaningful, self-developing internal computation—is broader than this candidate explanation.
