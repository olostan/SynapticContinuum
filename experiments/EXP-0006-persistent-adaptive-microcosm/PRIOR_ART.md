# EXP-0006 — What is and is not new (prior art, not host results)

Documented sources checked 2026-10-24. Literature evidence is distinct from our *unexecuted* hypotheses. Re-evaluate newer research prior to novelty claims.

1. **DreamerV3 (Hafner et al., Nature 2025)** — world model/actor/critic trained from replay, recurrent latent state and imagined futures; strong existing example of nonlinguistic latent world modeling and self-generated dynamics to improve real downstream performance. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2 . **Challenge to our novelty:** persistent latent world models already exist; we must isolate what *our* state persistence, adaptation, local plasticity or teacher withdrawal accomplishes that a competitive recurrent model does not.
2. **Growing Neural Cellular Automata (Mordvintsev et al., Distill 2020)** — local asynchronous-ish updates, learned persistence and regeneration; compelling evidence that local simple dynamics develop robust self-organization. https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/ . **Challenge:** persistence/repair alone is not relational induction or cognitive representation.
3. **MiniGrid/BabyAI (Farama)** — simple partial observation, boxes/keys/doors and configurably small causal environment with symbolic instructions. https://minigrid.farama.org/ ; https://github.com/Farama-Foundation/Minigrid . **Challenge:** small grid worlds with heldout goal tasks are not novel; world serves as instrument here, not a new benchmark leaderboard.
4. **Constitutional AI / RLAIF (Anthropic 2022)** — reinforcement from AI-generated evaluation and criticism; AI-guided learning not novel. https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073 . **Difference proposed:** LLM is asynchronous training-only evaluator/curriculum guide for a small nonlinguistic persistent student, NOT in the autonomous inference loop; test independent truth and withdrawal.
5. **Maass, Natschläger, Markram (2002): real-time computing without stable states / liquid state machines** — transient continuous recurrent spiking reservoirs can preserve temporal information useful for readout. https://doi.org/10.1162/089976602760407955 . **Challenge:** merely encoding past input in recurrent state is decades-old.
6. **Mongillo, Barak, Tsodyks (2008): synaptic theory of working memory** — memory may be stored in synaptic states without constant spiking. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1150769 . **Challenge:** high internal firing is not a necessary ingredient for continuity.
7. **EXP-0001** historical negative: silence processing without appropriate stability caused degradation; explicit timestamps dominated learning. ../EXP-0001-temporal-memory/REPORT.md
8. **EXP-0002** historical positive-limited: homeostatic recurrent activity persisted and interventions changed internal states/behaviors, but did not establish useful autonomous learning, concept formation or consciousness. ../EXP-0002-event-driven-dynamics/REPORT.md
9. **EXP-0003/4** historical engineering: validated native scheduling, direct CSR, queues, topology and memory feasibility in particular workload regimes; do NOT interpret inactive neuron allocation as cognition. ../EXP-0003-native-engine-scaling/REPORT.md ; ../EXP-0004-topology-adaptive-scheduling/REPORT.md
10. **EXP-0005** substrate work: canonical fixture/oracle and planned native scientific simulator evaluations; source of reusable harness ideas but does not determine EXP-0006 science. ../EXP-0005-scientific-simulation-substrates/SPEC.md

**Novelty claim presently allowed:** We are proposing a testable *combination and evaluation target*, not demonstrating a unique mechanism. No published work is disproved by an animation, nor by one small-world accuracy result. Need a strong time-aware recurrent/SSM control, a faithful truth oracle, teacher-budget control, causal intervention, and a growth/retention demonstration. If existing approaches match the student, record their superiority/equivalence and update research priorities.
