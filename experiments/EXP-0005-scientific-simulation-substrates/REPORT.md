## 4. Adversarial Falsification & Schema v2 Validator Results

All **33 unit tests** across `test_fixture_reproducibility.py`, `test_golden_oracle.py`, `test_falsification_adversarial.py`, and `test_schema_v2_validator.py` pass:

1. **Oracle Falsification Rejections (`test_falsification_adversarial.py`):**
   - Non-positive synaptic delay (`delay_ms = 0.0` or `-1.0`) $\implies$ rejected with `CausalityViolationError`.
   - Negative or backward event timestamps (`t_ms = -0.1` or `3.0 -> 1.0`) $\implies$ rejected with `CausalityViolationError`.
   - Corrupted fixture SHA-256 $\implies$ rejected with `ChecksumMismatchError`.
   - Mixed or wrong physical units (`delay_s`, `weight_V`, `time_unit='s'`) $\implies$ rejected with `UnitMismatchError`.
   - Perturbed Golden B weight (`w = 0.35 mV` instead of `0.40 mV`) $\implies$ falsifies threshold crossing at $t=4.0\text{ ms}$ (`0` spikes).
   - Grid-quantized arrival timestamp (`t = 1.00 ms` vs `1.04 ms`) $\implies$ shifts $V(3.0^-)$ by $> 10^{-4}\text{ mV}$, breaking analytic tolerance and output trace SHA-256.
2. **Schema v2 Validator & End-to-End Provenance Gates (`test_schema_v2_validator.py` & [`runs/wp00-validator-dryrun/validation_report.json`](runs/wp00-validator-dryrun/validation_report.json)):**
   - **Fail-Closed Manifest Gate:** Rejects validation when `fixtures_manifest` is `None` or missing on disk.
   - **Independent On-Disk Byte Hashing & Recipe Recomputation:** Hashes `config_path` and every referenced fixture CSV/JSON file on disk, verifies exact byte length and SHA-256 against the manifest, and recomputes `workload_instance_id` from `(workload_id, seed, config, file_hashes)`. Rejects tampered fixture bytes, tampered `config_sha256`, tampered recipe parameters, or deleted files.
   - **Custom On-Demand Fixture Round-Trip:** Verifies that `generate_custom_fixture_cli` (including default `modular_sbm` $\to$ canonical `sc05/modular/v1`) emits a compliant `sc05-fixture-manifest-v1` manifest that validates end-to-end through `validate_sc05_v2.py`.
   - **Fixed-`dt` Interoperability (`aligned-with-tolerance`):** Verifies that a fixed-step (`dt_ms = 0.1`) native model record with full 40-char commit SHA, `adapted_input_sha256`, and documented error metrics (`spike_time_mae_ms`, `membrane_rmse_mv`) passes under `Tier-B-observationally-aligned` / `aligned-with-tolerance`, while claiming `equivalent-exact` with `dt_ms = 0.1` is rejected.
   - **Zero-for-Unmeasured / Missingness Gate:** Rejects any `null` field lacking an explicit explanation in `missing_or_unsupported_fields`.
   - **Allocation-as-Activity & Optimistic PDES Gates:** Rejects zero-activity runs on active workloads and rejects `ROSS` records missing `rolled_back_events` or `reexecuted_events`.
