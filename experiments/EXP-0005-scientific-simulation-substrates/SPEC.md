# EXP-0005 — Scientific Simulation Substrates and Algorithm Exploration

**State: DESIGN ONLY / NOT RUN.** 2026-10-24. Scientific owner approval and independent design review required before execution. Governing protocol: [../../EXPERIMENTATION.md](../../EXPERIMENTATION.md). Do not modify historical EXP-0004 files.

## Mission / non-goal
Find simulation substrates that enable experimentally useful **long-running, adapting, causally consequential neural activity** under constrained computing resources. Performance is an enabling variable, not evidence of cognition. No simulator is presumed to produce cognition, to be biologically faithful, or to beat a domain-specific engine. Preserve null outcomes.

## Research questions and competing hypotheses
- **H1 (event vs clock costs):** At matched neural model, *actual processed work* and numerical tolerance, event/lazy integration is more efficient for sparse input, but time-stepped/vectorized updates cross over for sustained dense activity. **Falsifier:** no reproducible workload-dependent crossover, or advantage disappears after initialization and fidelity costs are accounted for. Test idle silence, Poisson, bursts, persistent feedback separately.
- **H2 (generated computation):** Brian 2 standalone compiled C++ can make richer continuous variables and plastic synapses tractable relative to interpreted execution. **Falsifier:** compilation overhead and generated runtime dominate at the fixed experiment horizons / plasticity depths. Test cold/e2e and warmed simulation separately, including multiple-run limitations.
- **H3 (cell grouping / model richness):** Arbor's recipe, cell group and optimized CPU execution may enable detailed per-neuron dynamics that flat event engines cannot represent natively; it may lose memory/throughput on point-LIF-only studies. **Falsifier:** no measurable advantage in its native domain after including model-state bytes and numerical tolerances. Do not equate cable compartments with neurons or synapses.
- **H4 (optimism / causality):** ROSS optimistic Time Warp can use otherwise lost parallel slack for feedback-heavy graphs with weak conservative lookahead; rollback, state saving and GVT can instead cause worse memory/time or nonreversible adaptive state. **Falsifier:** no proven rollback plus correct committed output, or no improvement over sequential/conservative reference in any matched LP workload. Report negative results even if event rate is high.
- **H5 (memory x temporal fidelity):** Compact per-connection history, lazy analytic decay, shared trace banks and/or event-triggered updates can expand the *active simulated horizon at fixed memory* while preserving task-relevant dynamics. **Falsifier:** savings vanish after complete state/monitor bookkeeping or material fidelity degradation. Do not conflate inactive allocation with activity.
- **H6 (long horizon):** A backend that works on a short benchmark may fail under hours of simulated time due to event queues, long-tail memory, plasticity instability, or recording overhead. **Falsifier of scalability claim:** recurrent traffic, spike count, update count or resident memory isn't sustained/observed; declaring allocation-only success is forbidden.
- **H7 (architecture transfer):** A demonstrably beneficial mechanism (e.g. delay-batched communication, compiler-generated kernels, reversible state) can be isolated and later tested as a *new*, separately validated experimental algorithm without adopting an entire framework. **Falsifier:** benefit depends on hidden simplification or unaccounted native semantic change.

These are proposed engineering hypotheses, not positive findings.

## Independent baselines / controls
1. **Mathematical oracle:** independently written tiny CPU reference for single-neuron leak and event delivery, analytical test sequences, and scalar STDP equations; must NOT call adapter under test or reuse EXP-0004's event-processing implementation. Validate oracle with hand-computable cases.
2. **Historical control:** EXP-0004 exact serial and optimized engines as EXTERNAL, pinned-build baselines, no inheritance of their neural engine classes by new native adapters. Its digests apply only to its own semantics and workload bytes.
3. **Framework self-controls:** NEST single-thread vs native multi-thread, Brian 2 runtime (NumPy/Cython if available) vs standalone C++, Arbor CPU lif vs cable-native detailed experiment, ROSS sequential/conservative vs genuine optimistic. Report supported variants rather than inventing compatibility.
4. **Workload controls:** no edges (dispatch only), disconnected regions (parallel ceiling), modular locality, random cross-worker stress, hub/bursty adversarial, quiet allocation control, time-step sweep, precision sweep, monitoring off/on.
5. **Scientific controls:** fixed/nonplastic vs STDP, history 0/short/long, clamped/external-only vs recurrent activity, shuffled time stamps and silent intervals, synthetic task readout with independent state intervention. None is evidence of cognition in isolation.

## Canonical numerical contract and comparison tiers
**Tier A analytic microfixtures:** continuous-time voltage equation dV/dt=-(V-E_L)/tau outside refractory, instantaneous weighted arrivals, threshold/reset, strictly positive axonal delay; compare to closed-form decay and hand-specified expected spikes. Time unit is **ms**, voltage **mV**, delta impulse **mV**, weight **mV**, τ=10 ms, E_L=0 mV, reset=0 mV, threshold=1 mV, refractory=2 ms unless native API forces a different physical parameterization. For test with arrivals (t=1 ms,w=0.4), (t=3 ms,w=0.4), pre-threshold V(3+)=0.4*exp(-2/10)+0.4; no spike; at (t=4 ms,w=0.4), V(4+)=V(3+)*exp(-1/10)+0.4 which exceeds 1, and spikes once. Hand-compute independent oracle and assert. No recurrent feedback for this initial test.

**Tier B observationally aligned:** native-LIF/threshold/decay or explicit ODE with same conceptual inputs, graph, weights and delay; align units, initial state, refractory, effective on-grid stimulus. First build a semantic mapping table: equation, solver, threshold sampling, reset, delivery rounding, simultaneous-event tie handling, monitor selection. Distinguish unsupported mapping. Use spike precision/recall with matching tolerance min(0.5 ms, 2*dt), spike-time MAE and 95th percentile, binned spike count RMSE, membrane RMSE at common sample times, trajectory divergence; **never claim bit parity** unless identical exact equations, ordering and representation were demonstrated. Compare at dt=0.1,0.05,0.01 ms where supported, and plot cost vs error. Fixed-dt and continuous-time are different fidelity classes.

**Tier C native-capability science:** framework-specific plasticity, cable, long-history, optimistic scheduler; no direct speed ranking unless common function and fidelity. Use Pareto plots in dimensions (event/horizon rate, measured active population, state bytes, peak RSS, fidelity, plasticity capabilities, horizon). Native capability value is recorded even with no same-model competitor.

## Scaling & workload axes (staged; NOT full factorial)
- Sizes N=128,1024,10000; optional N=100000 after evidence; further optional N=1M only approved on measured headroom, **no 10M minimum**. D=4,16,64, initial 16. Measure allocated N/M separately from active N and actual event/spike/synapse-update counts.
- Topology: disconnected, random fixed-outdegree, Watts–Strogatz-like directed small-world, modular SBM, hierarchical modular; preserve edge multiplicity/direction and graph digest. Real connectomes deferred to phase after synthetic validation.
- Activity: externally sparse 0.1–1% active, moderate 10%, dense 50%; held-out event replay; burst vs sustained 1/10/100 Hz presynaptic input with separate refractory and feedback controls. Actual downstream activity may differ; record instead of forcing identical counts.
- Simulated horizons 100 ms, 10 s, 100 s, optional 1 h only once stability/stop gate passes. Wall times and simulated times ALWAYS different fields.
- Temporal-history capacity 0, 8, 64 effective traces/entries; record per-neuron vs per-synapse allocation, compression, decay math, and cost of history readout. No artificial fixed-length history in frameworks lacking it; flag unsupported.
- Parallelism 1,2,4,6 physical cores, SMT 12 diagnostic only after sanity. Mac M4 Pro portable C++ baseline optional later; Metal/MLX after portable evidence, never assumed superior.
- Per primary comparison >=5 independently seeded fixtures (11,23,37,41,53) and >=5 repeated measured execution runs per fixture where cost permits. At least 2 non-timed warmups; randomized run order and full failure count. An expensive exploratory cell may use fewer reps, clearly demoted from confirmatory evidence.

## Predeclared gates and decision rules
- **G0 design:** native library semantics confirmed by official source/docs, version provenance captured; no installation or benchmark until design reviewed.
- **G1 functional:** 3 microfixture tests (leak, threshold, event delay) pass within documented tolerance; deterministic seed and trace reproducibility; no fabricated equivalence.
- **G2 reference:** independent oracle + at least one held-out short recurrent fixture; no backward-time committed events, no duplicate/lost events, no unbounded queue; platform-suitable sanitizers where code is owned.
- **G3 measurement:** cold build/setup/warm compute/simulation/readout separated; peak RSS and sustained actual counts, repetition array, git+source SHA, dependency versions and exit status present; OOM/timeouts retained as negative results.
- **G4 expand:** primary small and medium cells first; at 100k/1M preflight every run against min(60% of /proc/meminfo MemAvailable at launch, 6 GiB incremental RSS) and abort on safety threshold / swap growth. Hour-long wall-time single cells require owner approval.
- **G5 interpretation:** explanatory architecture evidence and matching fidelity profiles, not just winner ranking. Separate exact (only demonstrably identical), observationally aligned, approximate, feature-only and unsupported.
- **G6 synthesis:** independent audit of at least two adapters, reproduction of golden fixture, reconciliation with EXP-0004 atlas, explicit negative findings and recommendations.

**No claim thresholds are arbitrary magic:** compare uncertainty intervals, not just nominal speedup. For a positive substrate result require a reproducible >=1.5x improvement in one matched constrained regime *or* demonstrable native scientific capability absent from baseline with acceptable resource/fidelity cost, and no hidden complexity downgrade. This is a decision heuristic, not statistical significance. Null/negative evidence still passes scientific reporting.

## Falsification and statistical audit
Pair seeds / fixture bytes when semantics permit, report medians, IQR, range, bootstrap 95% intervals for paired log-runtime ratios, raw repetition arrays, all crash/failed counts, activity trajectories not just totals. Stratify compilation, construction, simulation, output; normalize per actual events only when event definitions match and report simulated seconds per wall second. Always include absolute end-to-end cost. Do not compare simulated-rate values from incompatible network time scales. Predeclare confidence interpretation, do not p-hack top cells. If spontaneous dynamics appear, use perturbed-state and frozen/sleep controls and test causal behavioral readouts separately.
