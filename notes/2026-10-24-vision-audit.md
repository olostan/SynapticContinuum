## Verdict

**Replace the *conceptual center*, not the empirical history.** The initial repository rightly emphasized token-free persistent neural dynamics, temporal connections, endogenous activity and optional language. But it did not adequately articulate:

1. open-ended, recursively interacting possibilities rather than one conclusion or output;
2. retrospective explanations, uncertain present, future branches and counterfactuals with **provenance**;
3. distributed experiences and contingent object/word interpretation, including *delayed naming*;
4. partial matching of temporal histories, analogy, causal role binding and avoiding inappropriate trait transfer;
5. multidimensional **significance/appraisal**, learnable priorities and finite exploration budget;
6. internally selected information-gathering actions and the ability to rest;
7. meaning emerging before human-language decoding, without forbidding symbolic controls;
8. self-development of not just memories/weights but the **policy of what deserves thought**;
9. loss of information/decoding illusions, self-confirming imaginative errors and suppression-collapse;
10. nearest rich prior art (Copycat/Metacat, MicroPsi, NARS, intrinsic motivation, constructive memory, distributed coding, active inference).

The earlier docs sometimes promoted one scientific mechanism (temporal response kernels) to center stage before the wider vision was articulated, and navigation made a timed-greeting proposal feel like the entry test of artificial cognition. This revision corrects *orientation*, not the original measurements.

## What this PR explicitly does NOT do

- It does not abandon temporal neural connections, embeddings, knowledge graphs, symbolic inference or pretrained models; it demotes all from *mandates* to *candidate mechanisms*.
- It does not assert that consciousness is present, artificial feelings are equivalent to human affect, or a particular developmental human's mind operates like our proposed models.
- It does not create experiments, claim that prior experiments demonstrated semantics, schedule research, run simulations, introduce neural code, change a world oracle or merge an experimental PR.
- It does not establish a new novelty claim; the broad goals have close ancestors in decades of cognitive architecture research.
- It does not ask agents to generate hidden narratives as 'thought'. The system may have no human-readable concept or word for a useful latent regularity.

## Questions requiring discussion before another implementation campaign

A. How can a compact system develop meaning from *experience* without a language decoder doing all the semantic work?

B. Should alternative imaginations be explicit branches, distributed superpositions, attractors, probabilistic samples, or all valid testable variants?

C. What is the minimal independently falsifiable demonstration of **unsolicited multi-step causal recombination**, rather than recalling a trajectory or executing a fixed simulator rule?

D. Can a learnable multidimensional significance mechanism make exploration more productive than fixed temperature, random search or one scalar intrinsic reward?

E. How do we preserve provenance and update beliefs about past/present/future without converting imagination into external 'facts'?

F. Which sensory inputs and minimal prior structure make object-like identity, role binding and analogy possible without predefined human concept nodes?

G. Do external scalar or LLM-based critics help ongoing development, or suppress unusual but promising trajectories? How do we assign temporal credit without becoming a teacher?

H. Which compact-world result could constrain a path to larger, open-ended lifelong real-world inputs, and which would remain a toy benchmark?

**Recommendation:** review and merge the vision separately if the owner agrees, then reconsider PR #16 against it. Design one small discriminating comparative study only after selecting *what causal phenomenon* matters, not because a detailed experiment folder already exists.
